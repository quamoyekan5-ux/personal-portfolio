About Me

A Creative Who Codes & Designs

Hi! I'm Quam Oyekan Alani, better known as Olamzy_Graphics — a passionate creative at the intersection of design, technology and Software Engineer. I blend artistic vision with technical skill to create digital experiences that are both beautiful and functional.

My journey started with a deep love for visual design — crafting logos, flyers, and brand identities. Over time, I fell in love with code, and today I'm growing into a Frontend and Web Developer who builds the interfaces I once only sketched.

I'm still learning, still growing — and that's exactly what fuels me. Every project is a new challenge, every line of code a new skill, every design a new story. My goal is to build a career as a full-stack creative — someone who can take an idea from concept to pixel-perfect, fully functioning reality.
